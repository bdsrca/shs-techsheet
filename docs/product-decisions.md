# Product Decisions

## Decision 1: no payment in v1

Payment sounds exciting, but it is not the first trust problem. The first problem is safer matching and communication. Payment adds disputes, refunds, fraud handling, escrow, seller payouts, and legal obligations. That should come after the basic marketplace loop works.

## Decision 2: public browsing before signup

A marketplace has to feel alive before people commit. Let students browse first. Ask for school email when they want to save, post, or message.

## Decision 3: broad meetup hints only

A listing needs area context, not a private address. Broad hints like campus area, library, or public landmark are enough.

## Decision 4: small public profiles

Public profiles should show only what helps the marketplace work: display name or handle, broad school label, optional grade level, general area, and listings.

## Decision 5: three listing status dimensions

Publication, moderation, and sale status are separate because they answer different questions.

## Decision 6: AI should be quiet

AI is an operations assistant. It should not become the product's judge, guarantee, or brand promise.
