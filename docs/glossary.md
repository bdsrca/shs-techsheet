# Glossary

**Browse first**: Users can inspect the marketplace before making an account.

**School-gated**: Posting, saving, and messaging require a supported school email domain.

**Inquiry**: A structured buyer-to-seller contact request tied to one listing.

**Thread**: Multi-message conversation that follows an inquiry.

**Publication status**: Seller-controlled state such as draft, published, paused, or archived.

**Moderation status**: Admin/safety state such as normal, flagged, under review, or removed.

**Sale status**: Buyer-facing state such as available, on hold, or sold.

**AI assisted review**: Background classification, guidance, or triage that supports a human decision.
