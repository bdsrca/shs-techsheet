# AI Assisted Operations

AI belongs in the background of this product.

## Good uses

### Listing guidance

When a seller saves or publishes a listing, AI can suggest clearer wording, safer meetup language, or missing details. The seller should see helpful guidance, not hidden enforcement logic.

### Report triage

When a report is filed, AI can summarize the issue, tag risk type, and help prioritize the admin queue.

### Chat safety signals

Chat review should be limited to trigger paths, such as reports or specific safety workflows. It should not become a public feature that implies constant surveillance.

## Guardrails

- budget caps
- rate limits
- retry limits
- hard stop behavior
- stored findings with reason and confidence
- admin control over final actions

## What AI should not do

- guarantee that a buyer or seller is safe
- act as emergency response
- silently remove accounts without review
- expose private risk labels to other students
- replace public meetup safety basics

The right posture is simple: AI helps the queue stay awake. People still decide.
